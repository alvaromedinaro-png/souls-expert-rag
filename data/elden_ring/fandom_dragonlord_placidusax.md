# Dragonlord Placidusax
The Dragonlord whose seat lies at the heart of the storm beyond time is said to have been Elden Lord in the age before the Erdtree. Once his god was fled, the lord continued to await its return. - Remembrance of the Dragonlord
Dragonlord Placidusax is a Remembrance Boss in Elden Ring, encountered in Crumbling Farum Azula.

## Characteristics

Dragonlord Placidusax is a quadrupedal dragon with two wizened heads and a grievously wounded gravel stone body. The scales protecting his head and back are drained of color, while those on his underbelly are vibrant gold. Each of his craggy, horn -strewn faces has yellow eyes, paired crown horns, and a large horn on the snout. They both have long strands of white hair that continue down the full length of their necks. In his prime, Dragonlord Placidusax had five heads, each emerging from the vortex-like swirl at the base of his neck, though now only two remain. He also had two pairs of wings in the past, but has since lost the rear set. His exposed flesh is red and gold.
Placidusax summons Red Lightning and can assume the form of a storming thundercloud. He can fire beams of golden flame from his both mouths.

## Background

In the prehistoric era, eons before the age of the Erdtree, the Lands Between were ruled from Farum Azula by Dragonlord Placidusax, the Elden Lord and many-headed king of the beastmen and undying Ancient Dragons. However, after he lost a head and his god fled, but likely before the meteorite struck his flying royal city, Placidusax retreated to the storm beyond time to dream in eternal anticipation of his god's return. He required his followers to render up their sleep and remain ever-awake, so that he might find solace in slumber. The timeless ancient dragons, to whom the passing of many lifetimes had rendered notions of ambition, hunger, and fierceness of spirit as utterly unfathomable, received his blessings and grace in return.
In time, the drake dragons —paltry, mortal offspring of the ancient dragons—rebelled, and Placidusax was betrayed by one of his own: Bayle the Dread. The old dragons formed a living wall of rock to protect their Dragonlord, but not before Bayle inflicted mutual, dire wounds upon the Dragonlord, claiming two of his heads and fleeing to the Jagged Peak. In retaliation, the Dragonlord marked Bayle and his bloodline for death. He reportedly slew the colossal drake and devoured its Dragon Heart, consummating the first Dragon Communion. He sent his followers to introduce Dragon Communion to mankind, within whom he recognized the mad hunger and fiery drive necessary to destroy Bayle.
Farum Azula became a mausoleum enshrining Placidusax, guarded by his chosen beasts. Despite his severely weakened condition, Placidusax continued to grant blessings to his followers. Several of Placidusax's Gravel Stone scales were disseminated throughout the world. They were used as smithing stones, said to allow for the creation of god-slaying weapons by lightly twisting time.

## Location

Dragonlord Placidusax can be found in the storm beyond time at Crumbling Farum Azula. The nearest site of grace is Beside the Great Bridge, from which it can be reached by traveling north and down the slope. The player needs to jump down from a broken section of the bridge to reach his mausoleum complex. Once inside, activate the empty beastman to lie down. The storm rewinds time, and the Dragonlord appears.
The arena is a large, circular platform with pillars around its perimeter. These can be destroyed by Placidusax's attacks. The size of the arena gives ample room to avoid his area of effect or dodge his divebomb attacks.
Defeating Dragonlord Placidusax unlocks the Dragonlord Placidusax site of grace, which appears inside the arena.

## Boss Fight


### Phase 1

Placidusax will begin the fight by summoning a storm of red lightning bolts which rain down on the arena for a few seconds. During his first phase, he will primarily use lightning claw attacks if the player is in front, a slam attack with his wings if they are to either side, and a tail sweep if they get behind him. He also has fire breath attacks which cover a large area in front of or around him. He has an area of effect attack which involves summoning a large lightning bolt and thrusting it into the ground, creating a massive explosion after a short delay. When he performs this attack, the music will briefly get quieter and red waves will emanate from where the lightning bolt hits, providing an indicator for the range of the explosion.

### Phase 2

Placidusax now gains the ability to disappear in a cloud of fog. If he floats into the air before doing so, he will reappear in a stationary or moving thundercloud above the arena, before emerging and diving down at the player. He also gains the ability to disappear and reappear in quick succession, unleashing attacks with his lightning claws in between.
