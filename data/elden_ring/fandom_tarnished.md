# Tarnished
Arise now, ye Tarnished. Ye dead, who yet live. The call of long-lost grace speaks to us all. - Narrator
The Tarnished (褪せ人, Asebito; lit. "Faded Ones/People") are a faction in Elden Ring. The player character, the Tarnished, is a member of this group.

## Overview

The Tarnished are individuals who lost the grace of the Erdtree and were consequently banished from the Lands Between by Queen Marika the Eternal. Following the breaking of the Elden Ring, the Tarnished were beckoned by lost grace, fragmentary divine light from the Erdtree, to return.

## Background

In the early age of the Erdtree, Godfrey, the first Elden Lord, fought many battles throughout the Lands Between. He led the War Against the Giants, and defeated the Storm Lord. When his last enemy fell, it is said that the hue of his eyes faded. Queen Marika addressed Godfrey and his warriors as she divested them of their Grace, commanding them to leave the Lands Between and travel to a faraway land, where they would wage war and die. She promised them that, after their deaths, their Grace would be given back, and that they would return to the Lands Between once again, to wage war and brandish the Elden Ring.

### The Long March

Godfrey and his kinfolk were banished from the Lands Between, beginning an exodus referred to as the Long March. They left their home on boats, although one was said to have been left behind. The warriors who accompanied Godfrey honored him by wielding axes, although Godfrey's own axe was broken in battle during the course of the march. When the Long March came to an end, Godfrey divested himself of kingship and became a simple warrior once more. As Hoarah Loux, he became a chieftain of the Badlands, revered by its bravest warriors.
The Tarnished settled in lands outside the Lands Between, and bore descendants who were also Tarnished. As Queen Marika had commanded them, the Tarnished lived and died in these distant lands.

### Return to the Lands Between

A long time after the Tarnished left the Lands Between, the Elden Ring was shattered and Queen Marika disappeared. Marika's offspring, the Demigods, claimed shards of the Elden Ring. Tainted by their newfound strength, the Demigods warred in the Shattering, causing the Greater Will to abandon them. With the war yielding no victor, the Guidance of Grace was extended to the Tarnished, who returned from the dead and went back to the Lands Between, guided by Grace. Upon setting foot in the Lands Between once more, the Tarnished received the aid of the Two Fingers, who bid them to seek the Great Runes possessed by the Demigods so as to restore the Golden Order and become Elden Lord. The Tarnished, given life by Grace, gained a form of immortality,, allowing them to return from the dead over and over on their quest to become Elden Lord.
The Guidance of Grace brought together the Tarnished and their Finger Maidens, women in the service of the Two Fingers who were tasked with serving their appointed Tarnished. The Finger Maidens would offer guidance and aid to the Tarnished, as well as turning the Runes they gathered into strength.

### Table of Lost Grace

Roundtable Hold became a gathering place for the Tarnished, and was where they sought audience with the Two Fingers. Among the first Tarnished to visit Roundtable Hold were Vargram the Raging Wolf and Errant Sorcerer Wilhelm. Vyke, a knight of the Roundtable Hold, came closer than any other Tarnished to becoming Elden Lord. However, without announcement, he disappeared below Leyndell and was scorched by the Frenzied Flame. At some point, the Cursemark of Death was recovered from the body of Godwyn the Golden and taken to the Roundtable. Fia, the Deathbed Companion came to the Roundtable in opposition where she siphoned the vigor of champions and developed Fia's Mist, a spell that only works against the Tarnished.
Although the Tarnished had once again been given Grace, some lost sight of it once more. At least one Tarnished, Roderika, never once saw the Guidance of Grace. With many Tarnished seemingly abandoned by Grace, an Elden Lord would not arise for a long time. Roundtable Hold, once the home of venerated warriors, stopped receiving visitors.

### Game events

This would change when a Tarnished of no renown crossed the fog and entered the Lands Between. Unlike others, this Tarnished did not meet a Finger Maiden to guide them, but was instead chosen by Torrent, the spectral steed, and Melina, a mysterious woman bound to Grace. Melina provided them with the Sacred Flasks and offered to serve as their maiden, aiding them by turning their Runes into strength. She extended an invitation to the Table of Lost Grace and bestowed the Spectral Steed Whistle to summon Torrent.
This Tarnished gained an audience with the Two Fingers, claimed the Great Runes, and journeyed to the Erdtree to brandish the Elden Ring and become the Elden Lord.
