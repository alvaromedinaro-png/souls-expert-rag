## Acquisition

Obtained after reaching the Stranded Graveyard at the start of the game.

## Effect

- Restores between 250 (at +0) and 810 (at +12) HP when used.
- Maximum charges are replenished after resting at a Sites of Grace.
- A charge can be expended to revive Torrent if he is killed. Torrent will be revived with half-health.
- Maximum charges can be increased using a Golden Seed at a Site of Grace. The number of seeds needed for each level increases as you progress.
- The potency of the flask can be permanently increased by expending Sacred Tears at a Site of Grace. The potency of the flask can be temporarily increased even further by equipping the following items: Crimson Tear Scarab (+10%) Guardian Garb (Full Bloom) (+10%) Crimson Seed Talisman / Crimson Seed Talisman +1 (+20%/+30%)
- Charges can be allocated between this flask and the Flask of Cerulean Tears at a Site of Grace.
