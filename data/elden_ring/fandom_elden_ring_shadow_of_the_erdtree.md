# Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree
Come, now. Touch the withered arm, and travel to the realm of shadow. I will not be far behind. - Needle Knight Leda
Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree is a downloadable expansion for Elden Ring. Created from the mythos written by George R. R. Martin, Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree expands Elden Ring with a sprawling new realm filled with additional characters, bosses, weapons, spells, and more. First announced on February 28, 2023, it released on June 20, 2024, and was the first DLC to be nominated for a Game of the Year Award.

## Background

Before Shattering the Elden Ring, Queen Marika sent her Demigod son on an unsung cleansing crusade against the land of the Tower, veiling both in a Realm of Shadow. It was to this land that Miquella the Kind departed, divesting himself of his flesh, his strength, his lineage, and of all things Golden. The Tarnished joins his followers, beckoned to trace the Empyrean 's painful pilgrimage through a land scorched by Messmer 's flame. Uncover the dark secrets of the Scadutree, and witness the birth of a new god at the heart of the Lands Between.

## Entering the Expansion

The player must defeat Starscourge Radahn and Mohg, Lord of Blood before they can interact with Miquella 's withered hand at the Cocoon of the Empyrean in Mohgwyn Dynasty Mausoleum to enter the expansion.

## Development

- Shadow of the Erdtree development began after the release of Elden Ring.
- George R. R. Martin did not create new material for the DLC; it was crafted from part of the original mythos he wrote for Elden Ring.
