# Stats and Attributes
Stats and Attributes determine the player character's gameplay-relevant properties. They can be inspected at the Character Status page, where the game separates them into the following categories:

## Attributes

These are the 8 attributes that can be increased by leveling up. Some affect base stats.

## Base Stats


## Attack Power

Depending on the weapon or shield wielded, a specific type of damage is done.
Note that Attack Power and damage dealt is not the same thing, with the latter depending how the former compares with defense. More specifically, if Attack Power is more than eight times as large as defense then damage dealt is equal to 90% of Attack Power while if Attack Power is less than an eighth of defense damage dealt is equal to 10% of Attack Power. If Attack Power is in between these breakpoints damage dealt falls somewhere between these two numbers.

## Guard Damage Negation

A weapon or shield's ability to reduce incoming damage when blocking.

## Damage Negation

Damage Negation (obtained through armor or items) reduces the damage of incoming attacks. Each Damage Type has a corresponding defense stat and damage negation stat that governs its mitigation.

## Defense

Defense (obtained through stats) also reduces the damage of incoming attacks.
