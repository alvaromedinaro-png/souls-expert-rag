# Mergo
Mergo is a mentioned character in Bloodborne.

## Description

Mergo is, or was, a infant Great One, likely to be the child of Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen and Oedon, a formless Great One.
The child appears to have been stillborn in some fashion, as evidenced by the item description of the Third Umbilical Cord dropped by Mergo (or Mergo's Wet Nurse). Mergo is all but stated to be the cause of the Nightmare of Mensis.
A pram implied to be housing the infant Mergo is found in the Wet Nurse's Lunarium located in Mergo's Loft Middle, guarded by Mergo's Wet Nurse, whose title implies she was appointed as Mergo's caretaker.
