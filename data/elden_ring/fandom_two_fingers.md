# Two Fingers
Fingers cannot speak, yet these were eloquent. Persistently did they wriggle, spelling out mysteries in the air. Thus did we gain the words. The words of our faith. - Two Fingers Heirloom
The Two Fingers are a set of NPC characters in Elden Ring. Seven pairs of Two Fingers can be found throughout the Lands Between.

## Characteristics

The Two Fingers are a large, hand-like creature with a slight indentation in its palm and twinned phalange-like appendages, hence their name. When standing upright, these fingers resemble heads with long and wispy white hair. Bowed with age, the Two Fingers are in a state of decay, with exposed flesh and lesions covering their ancient bodies.

## Background

The Two Fingers are the children of Metyr, Mother of Fingers, and serve the Lands Between as divine envoys of the Greater Will. They are responsible for selecting Empyreans, granting them shadowbound beasts, and guiding them toward godhood and inheriting the Elden Ring. They are the source of the Baleful Shadows and all Two Fingers incantations and possess the ability to restore blessings to Great Runes atop the ancient Divine Towers. They communicate in a cipher -like language of light composed of intangible symbols that hang in the air, which are perceived and translated by attending Finger Readers. In the past, the Fingers were masters of oration, their wisdom attracting champions from far and wide, establishing missionary churches in foreign realms beyond the Sea of Fog. However, their guidance became skewed long, long ago when their mother was damaged and consequently lost direct contact with the Greater Will.
In a prior age, a pair of Two Fingers deemed Marika an Empyrean and granted her Gurranq, Beast Clergyman. Under their guidance, she wove the Erdtree into existence from a Golden Seed and the primordial Crucible of life. They also selected Ranni, Malenia, Miquella, and the Gloam-Eyed Queen to be gods of the new age. After no clear victor emerged from the Shattering War, the Two Fingers abandoned the Demigods. Their favor was instead granted to the Tarnished, to whom the Grace of Gold had returned, issuing their instructions from the Roundtable Hold. Though most Tarnished follow their teachings, others doubt their judgment and have lost faith. They believe the Two Fingers to be corrupt and senile, having been broken along with the Erdtree long ago. The Two Fingers grant Darkness to confessors who hunt down these enemies.

## Locations

The first pair of Two Fingers can be found in Roundtable Hold, behind a set of doors that remain closed until the player Tarnished acquires their first Great Rune. They will be at the back of the chamber with Finger Reader Enia sitting close by. Five are found atop Divine Towers; however, each is dormant and lifeless. The seventh chose Ranni the Witch, and is concealed beneath the Cathedral of Manus Celes. She slays it with the Fingerslayer Blade during her questline to fully free herself from its control.
