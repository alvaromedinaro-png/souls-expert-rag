# Roundtable Hold
The Roundtable Hold, covert quarters of the Two Fingers, and gathering place of champions who vie to become Elden Lord. - Roderika
The Roundtable Hold is the main hub area in Elden Ring. Entry to Roundtable Hold is locked until the player receives an invitation from Melina, achieved by resting at a Site of Grace in Liurnia of the Lakes or Caelid, or by defeating Margit, the Fell Omen. Afterward, the Roundtable Hold can be accessed via fast travel from the map.

## Overview

A pocket realm tied to the Erdtree 's Grace, the Roundtable Hold exists to aid the Tarnished on their quest for the Elden Ring, with Smithing Master Hewg as its foundation. It is a copy of Leyndell 's Fortified Manor, once a gathering place for great champions seeking the Two Fingers ' divine wisdom. A bustling community of mighty Tarnished likewise convened in the Hold in the distant past; however, it is now a shadow of its former glory, with its present occupants lacking the venerable status and lofty aspirations of those past heroes, such as Vargram the Raging Wolf, Errant Sorcerer Wilhelm, Mad Tongue Alberich, Dolores the Sleeping Arrow, and Roundtable Knight Vyke, who came closest to the title of Elden Lord.

## Layout

Roundtable Hold consists of a main floor and a cellar, each comprised of several rooms. Certain rooms are inaccessible until certain requirements are met.

### Lobby

The central chamber of the Hold, containing the circular Table of Lost Grace site of grace, from which it gets its name. The player shall appear in this chamber every time they visit Roundtable Hold. Brother Corhyn, Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing, and Knight Diallos can all be found here initially, later joined by Roderika, Spirit Tuner and D, Hunter of the Dead. A Marika statue by the fireplace can be used to access the Colosseums, once unlocked.

### Northern Wing

The northern wing, ahead and to the left upon arriving in Roundtable Hold, consists of a corridor which branches into Gideon's study and the Twin Maiden Husks ' room. Ensha of the Royal Remains stands guard outside Gideon's study, and Nepheli Loux, Warrior briefly appears here after Godrick the Grafted has been defeated.

### Gideon's Study

A spacious room which has been adapted into a study by Gideon Ofnir, who can usually be found behind his desk. It will be locked upon first visiting Roundtable Hold, but will open either after claiming a Great Rune or talking to Gideon and reloading.

### Twin Maiden Husks

The Twin Maiden Husks can be found in a room through a door in the northern wing. Here, the player can buy and sell various items. They receive Bell Bearings, which allows them to sell the goods of dead merchants. Some of these Bell Bearings carry over into New Game Plus (NG+).

### Library

A small library behind a door in the Twin Maiden Husks' room, which will only open after touching a Site of Grace in Altus Plateau. The back of the library is piled with decaying corpses, and is occupied by the spirit of the Dung Eater.

### Eastern Wing

The eastern wing is a corridor which connects to Fia's room, the armory, and the cellar. Smithing Master Hewg resides here, and is later joined by Roderika.

### Fia's Room

A large bedroom occupied by Fia, Deathbed Companion. The Clouded Mirror Stand to the right upon entering can be used to open the Character Creation menu and alter one's appearance.

### Balcony

Situated between the northern and eastern wings, a balcony overlooks the great hall. Sorcerer Rogier retreats here after leaving Stormveil Castle.

### Hall

A large hall lined with banners and statues, accessed by dropping down from the balcony. Mad Tongue Alberich will invade when the player approaches the far end of the hall. After he has been defeated, a wisp of light will appear which allows the player to return to the balcony above.

### Living Quarters

A series of rooms connected to the hall, to the left after dropping down from the balcony. The Cipher Pata can be found on a corpse lying on one of the beds.

### Armory

A locked room at the far end of the eastern wing. It will only open after giving D the Weathered Dagger, received from Fia.

### Pantry

A storage room on the lower level, at the bottom of a flight of stairs past Hewg in the eastern wing. It is initially empty, but Nepheli Loux will move here after the Omenkiller in the Village of the Albinaurics has been defeated.

### Dining Room

A narrow room connected to the pantry, which requires a Stonesword Key to open. There is a treasure chest containing Crepus's Black-Key Crossbow and 20 Black-Key Bolts.

### Kitchen

A room connected to the dining room, requiring two Stonesword Keys to open. A treasure chest contains the Assassin's Prayerbook.

### Audience Chamber

A throne room connected to the lobby, wherein resides the Two Fingers and Finger Reader Enia. This chamber is locked until at least one Great Rune has been obtained. The player comes here to progress the main story and to trade in Remembrances for boss items.
