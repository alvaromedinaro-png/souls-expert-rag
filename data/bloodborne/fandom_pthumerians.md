# Pthumerians
The Pthumerians are a type of enemy in Bloodborne.

## Description

The Pthumerians are a race of humanoid beings and the inhabitants of the ancient Pthumeru, in addition to being a civilization that had contact with the Great Ones. They exhibit pale greyish skin, a slim body like a corpse and a head with a permanent, expressionless face, with their mouths wide open. Additionally, most of the Pthumerians possess tall and thin frames, though retaining great and powerful strength. Their strange, almost " undead-like " appearance stems from being marked by the Eldritch Truth when they came in contact with the Great Ones.
In-game, Pthumerians are powerful foes that comprise the bulk of the enemies found within the Chalice Dungeons, and are weak against Arcane damage.

## Lore

The Pthumerians were superhuman beings said to have unlocked the wisdom of the eldritch Truth. The old labyrinth carved out by them is known to be a place in which inquiry into the cosmos started. Early Pthumerians served as guardians of the Great Ones.
The Pthumerian monarch was traditionally a woman who assumed a name with classical roots; one of such monarchs was Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen. Both the monarch and its capital received a title of Pthumeru Ihyll.
