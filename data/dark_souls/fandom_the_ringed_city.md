# The Ringed City
The Ringed City is the second downloadable content for Dark Souls III. It was released on March 28th, 2017.

## Overview

At the close of the Age of Fire, as the world ends and all lands converge upon themselves, a lone adventurer descends into the madness of the earth and uncovers the secrets of the past.
The Ringed City is said to be at World's End, as far as one can go...

## Access

It can be accessed from the Kiln of the First Flame, a bonfire will appear at the start of the ramp leading to the Soul of Cinder arena. Alternatively, it can be accessed from a bonfire that appears in front of the statue at the back of Sister Friede 's arena in the Painted World of Ariandel.
