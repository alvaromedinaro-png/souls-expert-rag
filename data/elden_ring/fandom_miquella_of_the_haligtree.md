# Miquella of the Haligtree
I promise you. A thousand year voyage guided by compassion. - Miquella the Kind
Miquella the Kind, also known as Miquella of the Haligtree, Empyrean Miquella, and the Unalloyed, is a Remembrance Boss in the Elden Ring add-on Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree. He is an Empyrean and a son of Queen Marika the Eternal and King Consort Radagon. He is the older twin brother of Malenia, and together they are known as the Twin Prodigies (天賦の双子, Tenpu no Futago). His other self is St. Trina, whom he divested and cast into the depths of the Stone Coffin Fissure. He is encountered at the Gate of Divinity atop Enir-Ilim in the Realm of Shadow, where he fights alongside Radahn, Consort of Miquella.

## Background

Miquella and his twin sister were born with incurable afflictions, with Miquella's curse being perpetual youth. Miquella made a promise to Malenia and experimented tirelessly in an attempt to undo their curses. The twins were very close, and Malenia became Miquella's sworn blade and protector. Miquella was loved by many people and had a remarkable ability to compel affection from others. For this reason, many regarded him as the most fearsome Empyrean.
Just as his parents were two halves of the same person, Miquella also had another half— St. Trina —who held the power of Sleep. St. Trina was Miquella's fate, and she was his "love".
Miquella was chosen by his Two Fingers to become an Empyrean, destined to succeed Queen Marika. He was an adherent of Golden Order Fundamentalism, and developed new incantations as gifts to his father Radagon. However, Miquella abandoned Fundamentalism when it failed to treat Malenia's rot. He then began creating Unalloyed Gold, which he used it to craft needles that could ward away the meddling of outer gods and forestall Malenia's incurable rot. These needles could even be used to subdue the power of the Frenzied Flame. He also unsuccessfully sought a solution for his half-brother Godwyn 's state of incomplete death.

### The Haligtree

At some point, Miquella started watering a sapling with his own blood in the hope that it would become a new Erdtree. The sapling was grown on top of the fortress of Elphael and became known as the Haligtree. Soldiers and Knights sworn to protect the Haligtree would use unalloyed gold to symbolize their loyalty and devotion to Miquella, wearing braided crowns made of the metal and creating shields embedded with it.
Miquella was also said to favor the low and meek, and his Haligtree was intended to be the eventual salvation for the many. It became a hidden haven for the persecuted and the unwanted of the Lands Between, such as the Misbegotten and Albinaurics.
As Miquella pursued godhood, he knew that he would have to take a consort, to which his eyes fell upon his half-brother General Radahn, for he was strong but remained kind. He approached Radahn with his naive offer, to which the General vowed that he would become as such, in return for unspecified terms on Miquella and Malenia's behalf.
Malenia journeyed with her armies to Caelid to confront Radahn. The resulting Battle of Aeonia ended with Malenia unleashing her Scarlet Aeonia, whispering beforehand in Radahn's ear that "Miquella awaits thee, O promised consort." After using her last resort attack, Malenia entered a comatose state, and one of her knights took it upon herself to carry her leader's slumbering body back across the Lands Between to the Haligtree. Radahn had survived, albeit barely standing, and his mind was riddled by the Scarlet Rot. He was now little more than a howling beast aimlessly wandering the battlefield, devouring the corpses of friends and foes alike.
Following the Battle of Aeonia, Miquella embedded himself into the Haligtree to progress his ascension, but was kidnapped during his slumber. Another of Miquella's half-brothers was to blame: Mohg, who served an Outer God known as the Formless Mother. He took the sleeping Empyrean deep underground to the Mohgwyn Palace, where he aspired to raise Miquella to full godhood himself through blood offerings, and then to take on the role of Miquella's consort instead of Radahn, becoming the new monarch of a Mohgwyn Dynasty. Mohg urged his followers to render offerings of blood and drench Miquella's cocoon in order to facilitate his awakening.
Despite all of Mohg's efforts, Miquella remained completely unresponsive. At some point, Miquella had covertly placed an enchantment on Mohg, wishing to use the Omen to gain access to the Realm of Shadow. Sir Ansbach, a Pureblood Knight in service to Mohg, attempted to free his lord by challenging Miquella, only to succumb to an enchantment of his own.
Without Miquella's blood offerings, and Malenia no longer protected by her Unalloyed Gold Needle once she was returned, the Haligtree became a misshapen husk. slowly succumbing to the unmitigated Scarlet Rot. Malenia and the Haligtree's soliders desperately awaited Miquella's return to save their rotting home.

### The Realm of Shadow

My loyal blade. And champion of the festival. Both your deeds will ever be praised in song. Now, the vow will be honoured, and my Lord brother's soul will return. So that he may be my consort. - Miquella the Kind
Making use of Mohg, Miquella entered the Realm of Shadow, the location of the Gate of Divinity. As Miquella traveled through the Realm of Shadow, he abandoned parts of himself, including his flesh, his eye, his doubt and vacillation, his love, and his fears. Golden Miquella's Crosses resembling a Great Rune appeared at each location where he abandoned something, akin to Miquella's footprints. Miquella's followers, who pursued him into the Realm of Shadow, used these crosses to trace his path through the realm. Miquella also discarded his other half, St. Trina, in the Stone Coffin Fissure. St. Trina, whose love for Miquella was boundless despite her abandonment, recognized that godhood would be a prison for the Empyrean, and desired for him to be killed out of mercy.
Eventually, a Tarnished champion broke into Mohg's palace and slew the Lord of Blood for his great rune. Sir Gideon Ofnir suggested it might be safer to destroy the cocoon that Miquella remains asleep in. Instead, the Tarnished met Needle Knight Leda, a loyal follower of Miquella, who urged them to travel to the Realm of Shadow by touching Miquella's withered arm within the cocoon.
Within the Realm of Shadow, the Tarnished encounters several more of Miquella's followers, learns of his mission to ascend to godhood via the Gate of Divinity, and of the powerful charm that he had placed upon his disciples, which is broken when he discards his Great Rune. After gaining access to the tower of Enir-Ilim, the Tarnished is confronted by a resurrected Radahn, in a vessel made from the remains of Mohg. It is unclear whether Miquella always intended to use Mohg's body to resurrect Radahn. However, he needed a vessel, likely a hornsent or omen one due to their strong connection to the spiritual world, to bind to Radahn's soul. During the ensuing battle, Miquella steps out of the Gate of Divinity and embraces Radahn as his consort. The Tarnished triumphs over Radahn and Miquella, thus preventing Miquella's age of compassion.

### Cut Content

- In earlier versions of Elden Ring, Miquella was the shardbearer of the Rune of Abundance. In one version, Miquella was associated with the Abundance Twinblade, an item which was later released as Euporia in Shadow of the Erdtree. In another build of the game, Miquella's consciousness would have been within the world of his dream. In a cut quest, Miquella acted as St. Trina of the Cradlesong, the saint of slumber. He would have sung a lullaby to soothe the Red-Eyed Merchants of their Frenzied Flame affliction and helped them to retain their personhood and prevent them from becoming "one".
- A cutscene associated with Miquella was cut from the final version of Elden Ring., with associated materials including Leaf, Root, Root_Large, Soil_Blood_Stain, Seedling_Blood_vessel, Seedling_Blood_vessel_branch, and Seedling_Blood_vessel_MultColor_branch. There is a cut dialogue most likely associated with this cutscene: "Young seedling, young seedling. Return to the bosom of earth. But remember well, Thou'rt mine. So shall I give of myself. This is for thee. Mine abundance, my drop of dew. Quench thy thirst, throughout thy frame. Blossom and burgeon, time and and time again. Grow larger, stronger. Until the day cometh. When thou canst share in my dream. Elden Ring, O Elden Ring. Beget Order most elegant, from my tender reverie." There is a separate dialogue, possibly related to the use of Miquella's Needle: "If thou covetest the throne, Impress my vision upon thine heart. In the new world of thy making, all things will flourish, whether graceful, or malign."
- There is cut dialogue in which Miquella would have intervened in support of Malenia; The phase one cutscene had her say: "Sweet Tarnished.. Dearest companion... Did you not heed my warning? Your greed knows no end. You would steal the last drop of warmth from his empty frame? After all you've taken, you still want more? Then you will have to kill me. I am Malenia, Sword of Miquella. And I have never known defeat." During her second phase cutscene, Miquella would've said: "My dear twin, accept this gift. A gift of abundance, my last drop of dew. Let all things flourish, whether graceful, or malign." Presumably, this drop of dew would allow Malenia to ascend to her second phase, and would've been a prerequisite of some kind for Miquella's cut cutscene.
- In Shadow of the Erdtree, there is an unused alternate set of dialogue for the Promised Consort Radahn cutscenes, sandwiched between the retail dialogue internally; Radahn's phase 1 cutscene would have him say: " I am Radahn. Born of red-maned Radagon, and Rennala of the Full Moon. A lion bred for battle." The phase 2 cutscene would have had Miquella say: "I hereby swear. To every living being, and every living soul. Now comes the age of our eden. A thousand year voyage guided by compassion. Beginning here, love encompasses all. No living thing will be denied, no deed censured. I am ready. To embrace the whole of it. Let us go together. Radahn, my promised consort."
