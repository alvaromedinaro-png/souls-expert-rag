# Godrick the Grafted
I command thee, kneel! I am the lord of all that is golden! - Godrick the Grafted
Godrick the Grafted ( 接ぎ木のゴドリック, Tsugiki no Godorikku) is a Boss in Elden Ring. He is a Demigod and a Shardbearer encountered in Stormveil Castle.

## Background

Godrick is a descendant of the Golden Lineage, the bloodline that began with Queen Marika the Eternal and her first consort Godfrey. Only a distant relation of Marika's line, his divine blood was sorely diluted, and he was viewed as the runt of the litter. Godrick idolized the first Elden Lord, desiring his ancestor to witness his feats. He wielded an axe emblazoned with a lion, a symbol of Godfrey, and furnished his army with the Erdtree and Serosh emblems of the Golden Lineage. Though he styled himself “lord of all that is golden,” he commanded far less respect than his forebear and was despised by many of his own servants.
After the Elden Ring was shattered, Godrick inherited the Great Rune from the center of the Elden Ring, referred to as the anchor ring. During The Shattering, he was hounded from Leyndell, Royal Capital. He claimed a multitude of treasures, including the Mimic's Veil, and hid among the womenfolk to flee the city. Godrick made his way south, settling in Limgrave with an army of soldiers who had fled Leyndell. He took up residence in Stormveil Castle, using it as a refuge to hide from General Radahn. When Malenia marched southward, Godrick insulted her, leading to a confrontation that left him beaten and humiliated, forced to beg for mercy.
Godrick again retreated to Stormveil Castle to practice grafting, the forbidden art of attaching the parts of other beings to oneself to gain power. When the Tarnished returned to the Lands Between, Godrick sent his soldiers on Tarnished hunts, seeking new subjects to graft. He also produced Grafted Scions; abhorrent beings comprised of many different limbs, seemingly grafted onto children.

### Game events

The Tarnished journeys to Stormveil Castle in search of Godrick's Great Rune, on the advice of White Mask Varré and Melina. The castle shows many examples of his grafting: severed limbs hanging in the dining hall, a dismembered Troll over a courtyard, and piles of corpses, including the companions of Roderika. Eventually the Tarnished finds Godrick in a graveyard, addressing a dead dragon impaled on a statue. He grafts the dragon's head to his own arm mid-fight, but is defeated, and his Great Rune is claimed.
Later, Nepheli Loux can become the lord of Limgrave at the appointment of Kenneth Haight.

## Boss Fight


### Overview

- HP: 6,080
- Defense: 105 (increased during Phase 2)
- Stance: 105
- Cannot be Parried
- Vulnerable to a Critical Attack after his Stance is broken
- Deals Standard and Strike damage during Phase 1; Phase 2 adds Fire damage via the Grafted Dragon.
- Drops: 20,000 runes, Remembrance of the Grafted, Godrick's Great Rune
- Nepheli Loux, Warrior may be summoned if spoken to in Stormveil; her Summon Sign is outside the arena.

### Resistances


### Moveset


### Phase 1

The fight begins after a cutscene. Godrick primarily uses his axe and enlarged left arm, generating cyclones and quakes for area-of-effect damage. He rolls to reposition and uses a homing wind projectile.

### Phase 2

At roughly 60% HP, a cutscene plays, and he grafts the dragon's head to his arm. He gains ranged and area fire attacks layered onto his Phase 1 kit.

### Environment

The arena is a narrow, paved approach flanked by graveyards, with a wider entrance near the steps. Movement through gravestones is possible, but it can hinder dodging.

## Grafted Monarch


### Boss Fight

He is fought as a challenge at the end of each day. However, he can only be encountered after Day 1 of both the Augur, Fissure in the Fog, and the Night Aspect expeditions.
