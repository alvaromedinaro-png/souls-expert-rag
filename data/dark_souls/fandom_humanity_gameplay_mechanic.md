# Humanity (gameplay mechanic)
Humanity is a gameplay mechanic in Dark Souls.
In-Game Description

## Overview

Humanity serves many functions and comes in two forms; hard Humanity is the consumable item, players may use these items and make your Humanity soft/held. It is a best practice to keep Humanity in its hard consumable form until you need it. Players will gain soft humanity sometimes while clearing a level while the area boss is still alive. Ten soft Humanity can be gained from most but not all areas if players are willing to dedicate an excessive amount of time to this. Players may also gain soft humanity and heal by consuming a humanity, twin humanities, and will gain 5 humanity from a Firekeeper Soul. Consuming Firekeeper Souls is not recommended.
Players can use hard Humanities as a healing item if needed, but be aware that when you die and then lose your bloodstain you will lose all soft Humanity in that bloodstain. It is a much better idea to use Humanity to restore your human form to kindle bonfires and never use it as a healing item. Always have a plan for your humanity and never consume a Firekeeper Soul to heal or gain Humanity. It is more worthwhile to give any Firekeeper souls you find to a Firekeeper to boost the amount of HP your Estus will restore. The number of soft humanity you have at any given time is displayed at the top left of your screen inside a circle by your HP and Stamina bar.

### Functions of Held Humanity

- When players have soft humanity the best thing to do with it is restore human form at a bonfire and kindle bonfires for more Estus Flask charges. When human form is restored, the game opens up to NPC and player invasions as well as co-op. Bonfires that are kept by a Firekeeper automatically grant ten Estus upon resting at them. By default any bonfire can be kindled to 10 Estus. Once players beat Pinwheel and gain the Rite of Kindling Bonfires will be able to be kindled to 20 Estus. These bonfires will remain at 20 Estus even when in next new game cycle.
- 10 held Humanity caps your total item discovery coupled with the Covetous Gold Serpent Ring for item farming purposes.
- Humanity increases the power of Chaos weapons (up to 10 humanity)
- Increases Curse resistance (up to 30 humanity)
- Having Humanity in its soft form also allows you to use it to rank up in the Chaos Servant and Darkwraith Covenants.

## Gaining Humanity

Players may use the item Humanity and the similar Twin Humanities to gain one or two points of humanity, respectively. Humanity is randomly acquired in an area up to a predetermined amount as long as players haven't killed the area boss or helped the host defeat the area boss as a White Phantom. Red Phantoms leave a bloodstain worth one humanity upon defeat, with the exception of Marvelous Chester ( AotA). The Dark Hand 's strong attack steals humanity from other players and some NPCs.

## Farming Liquid Humanity

In some areas, before the boss is defeated, certain enemies will increase your humanity count after many of them are killed. These enemies usually have one thing in common; they were once human, but are now undead and hollow. After a set number of an enemy type is killed, one humanity will be absorbed. Some enemies require fewer kills than others, as they have different values of 'humanity worth'.
- A total of 10 humanities can be absorbed in one area while the boss is still alive.
- The first humanity drops when the 'humanity worth' meter is built up to 100, the next drops at 105, third at 110 -- and the tenth drops at 300 (+200%). As an example: For a creature with a 'humanity worth' of 6, you have to kill 17 for the first humanity, 18 for the second, 19 for the third -- and finally, 50 kills for the tenth and last humanity drop.

## Using Humanity

Humanity can be spent at bonfires to reverse Hollowing or to kindle bonfires. Reversal of hollowing allows players to summon other players and invade worlds; kindling increases the maximum amount of Estus Flask charges a bonfire provides. Humanity is also needed to level up in the Chaos Servant and Darkwraith Covenants. Having a higher Humanity count increases resistance to Curses and increases attack power with Chaos weapons. However, only 1-10 humanity will affect the attack power of Chaos weapons.
