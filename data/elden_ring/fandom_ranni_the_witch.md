# Ranni the Witch
I am the witch Ranni. I stole Death long ago, and search now for the dark path. That I might one day upend the whole of it, and rid the world of all that came before. - Ranni the Witch
Ranni the Witch, also known as Lunar Princess Ranni and Renna the Witch, is a character in Elden Ring. She is one of the Demigods, and the focus of one of the game's most elaborate questlines.

## Background

A child born of the union between Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon and red-haired Radagon during the age of the Erdtree, Ranni embodied an alliance between Raya Lucaria and Leyndell. She had two older brothers, Radahn and Rykard. As a daughter of the Carian Queen, she was a Lunar Princess, and first heir in the royal line. Led by the hand of her mother, young Ranni encountered her Dark Moon —cold, dark, and veiled in occult mystery.
The Two Fingers named Ranni as an Empyrean, one with the potential to one day succeed Queen Marika as the divine ruler of the Lands Between, and granted her a Shadowbound Beast named Blaidd to be her vassal. He was approved of by Rennala and was raised as her foster brother. Ranni grew up playing with Blaidd, and they were often accompanied by Iji, a troll blacksmith who served the Carian royals.
At some point, Ranni's father abandoned her mother to marry Queen Marika and assume the title of second Elden Lord. Although Ranni and her siblings were raised to Demigod status by their new stepmother, their heartbroken mother was left indisposed, cradling her Amber Egg in all-consuming grief.
Ranni later ventured deep in woods, where she encountered a snowy crone who became her secret mentor. She was an old witch well versed in cold-based sorceries, and she taught Ranni to fear the Dark Moon as she imparted her magic. The full Dark Moon, cold and leaden, would become Ranni's sigil and symbol of her power and authority.

### The Night of Black Knives

Although the Two Fingers had chosen her as Marika's successor, Ranni did not wish to be a puppet for the Greater Will and resented her Empyrean fate. In order to escape it, Ranni stole a fragment of the Rune of Death from Maliketh, the Black Blade and, through a fearsome rite, imbued it into the blades of Numen assassins, who were said to be close to Queen Marika herself. She also gave a fragment to her brother Rykard as a last resort, so he could kill Maliketh if necessary. Ranni plotted for the assassins to kill another individual while she simultaneously killed herself, slaying her own body while the other person died in spirit, thus freeing herself from being an Empyrean.
In what would become known as the Night of Black Knives, the assassins murdered Godwyn the Golden, killing him in spirit, while Ranni killed herself in body, transferring her spirit into a doll said to be modeled after the image of her mentor, the Snow Witch. This marked the step-siblings as the first demigod casualties in history. Ranni's fate and her involvement in the plot remained largely unknown by the denizens of the Lands Between.

### The Shattering

At some point after the Night of Black Knives, Queen Marika shattered the Elden Ring. Ranni claimed a Great Rune, but appears to have discarded it, and largely remained outside of the conflict that followed. As a consequence of her doll body, Ranni required extended periods of hibernation and withdrew from the world, hiding in the Carian Manor while her agents worked to bring about her plan of ridding the Lands Between of the influence of the Greater Will, allowing her to pursue her divine ascension on her own terms.

### Game events

After the Shattering, Ranni learned of a recently arrived Tarnished riding the spectral steed, Torrent. She approached them at the Church of Elleh, inquiring whether they possess the steed that once accompanied Melina. Upon confirming her suspicions, she bestowed upon them a Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes, explaining that Torrent’s former master had entrusted her with delivering these items to its next owner.
Ranni later reunited with the Tarnished at her Rise in the Three Sisters, where she invited them into her service. She tasked the Tarnished with aiding Blaidd in uncovering a path into Nokron, Eternal City, to claim its treasure—a weapon capable of slaying the Two Fingers who had chosen her as an Empyrean. However, the path to Nokron would only reveal itself with the death of her brother, Starscourge Radahn, who had long held the stars —and thus Ranni's fate—in stasis with gravity magic.
After Radahn's defeat and the acquisition of the Fingerslayer Blade, Ranni descended to the Ainsel River Main, leading to Nokstella, Eternal City. There, she commanded her champion to rid her of the Baleful Shadows sent by the Two Fingers to assassinate her. Upon their victory, Ranni rewarded the Tarnished with a key to a Carian chest containing the Dark Moon Ring. Then, Ranni departed to fulfill her dark purpose. She traveled to the Cathedral of Manus Celes on the Moonlight Altar, where she slew the great Two Fingers beneath it and abandoned her doll body.
If the Tarnished retrieved the Dark Moon Ring and presented it to Ranni's doll, she accepted them as her consort, granting them the Dark Moon Greatsword as a symbol of their union. She revealed the full extent of her plan: to succeed Queen Marika as god and bring about the Age of the Stars. Ranni intends to remove the Elden Ring from the Lands Between and journey into the cosmos, freeing its inhabitants from the influence of the Greater Will and granting them true autonomy over their lives.
After defeating the Elden Beast, the Tarnished may summon Ranni, who ushers in the Dark Moon and her Age of the Stars, realizing her vision for a new Order.

## Characteristics

Ranni's disembodied spirit inhabits the woven puppet of a four-armed maiden with pallid blue skin and long, flowing blue hair. Her left eye—the doll's right—is sealed shut by an azure sigil, while both open eyes glow cyan. She wears an elegant white gown beneath a voluminous fur cloak, lined with two long brown braids. A massive, floppy-brimmed witch’s hat rests atop her head, crowned with a delicate silver filigree. Luminious blue veinwork runs throughout the frozen brim's cold underside.

## Ranni's Quest

- Completing Ranni's quest will result in the " Age of the Stars " ending.
- Her questline intersects with the following NPCs: Blaidd the Half-Wolf, Castellan Jerren, Fia, Deathbed Companion, Pidia, Carian Servant, Preceptor Seluvis, Smithing Master Iji, and Sorcerer Rogier.

### Questline progression

- (Optional) Church of Elleh After meeting Melina and obtaining the Spectral Steed Whistle, use Torrent at least once. After that, rest on any Site of Grace. Then, fast travel to the Church of Elleh Site of Grace and find Ranni (using the pseudonym " Renna the Witch ") sitting on a broken wall. Exhaust her dialogue to receive the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes for free. If you miss this step, these items will be available for purchase at the Twin Maiden Husks in Roundtable Hold — 200 runes for the Lone Wolf Ashes and 1,000 runes for the Spirit Calling Bell respectively.
- Caria Manor Traverse Caria Manor in northwestern Liurnia of the Lakes. Defeat the apparition of Royal Knight Loretta at the Royal Moongazing Grounds, which unlocks the path to Three Sisters behind her.
- Ranni's Rise Ranni can next be encountered on the top floor of her Rise in the Three Sisters. This stage varies depending on whether you have met her before, defeated Starscourge Radahn, or given Sorcerer Rogier the Black Knifeprint. Agree to become one of Ranni's vassals, and then head back down the tower to speak to the projections of Iji, Blaidd, and Seluvis. After speaking to all three, return to Ranni and speak to her again. After this point, if you return to Ranni, she will be sleeping and non-responsive. (Optional) Administer Preceptor Seluvis 's Amber Draught to Ranni. She will awaken. Speak to her three times, and she will banish you from her sight. WARNING: This will permanently end Preceptor Seluvis and Pidia, Carian Servant 's quests. WARNING: Ranni's questline will no longer progress; however, this can be undone by using Celestial Dew to receive absolution at the Church of Vows.
- Caelid Defeat Starscourge Radahn to resume the movement of the stars.
- Nokron, Eternal City Nokron can only be accessed via the Starfall Crater in east Limgrave after defeating Starscourge Radahn. Proceed through the area to obtain the Fingerslayer Blade, which is in a chest northeast of the Night's Sacred Ground Site of Grace.
- Three Sisters Travel to Ranni's Rise and deliver the Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni, who will give you the Carian Inverted Statue. This key item is required to progress Fia, Deathbed Companion 's questline. However, it is not necessary to complete Ranni's quest. WARNING: This will permanently end Preceptor Seluvis and Pidia, Carian Servant 's quest. Travel to Renna's Rise, which unseals after Ranni departs with the Fingerslayer Blade. Enter and climb to the top, and go through the Sending Gate to the upper portion of Ainsel River.
- Ainsel River Main Pick up the Miniature Ranni near the spawn point and rest at the Ainsel River Main Site of Grace. Use the "Talk to miniature Ranni" option three times to get her to speak.
- Nokstella, Eternal City (Optional) Ranni will also have dialogue at the Nokstella, Eternal City and Nokstella Waterfall Basin Sites of Grace. These are not required to continue her questline. Defeat the Baleful Shadow invader just past the Nokstella Waterfall Basin Site of Grace to obtain the Discarded Palace Key.
- Academy of Raya Lucaria Defeat Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon at the Raya Lucaria Grand Library. Unlock the chest behind Rennala and obtain the Dark Moon Ring.
- Lake of Rot Ride the Transportation Coffin at the Grand Cloister. Defeat Astel, Naturalborn of the Void and then travel up the lift. However, this lift remains locked until the player obtains the Dark Moon Ring.
- Moonlight Altar Travel to the Cathedral of Manus Celes and descend through the tunnel near the altar to find Ranni sitting on the corpse of her Two Fingers. Interact with her to trigger a cutscene. After the cutscene, speak to her again, and she will disappear, bequeathing the Dark Moon Greatsword.
- Stone Platform After defeating the final boss, there will be a blue summon sign beside Fractured Marika. Interact with it to summon Ranni, allowing you to achieve the Age of the Stars ending. WARNING: Ranni's summon sign will not appear if the player has met with the Three Fingers. This can be undone by defeating both Malenia, Goddess of Rot and Dragonlord Placidusax, and using Miquella's Needle in the latter's boss arena at Crumbling Farum Azula.
