# Erdtree
Ohh, Erdtree, great Erdtree. The Golden Order itself, unwavering, stretched to the sky. - Stranded Soul
The Erdtree (黄金樹, Ōgonju; lit. "Golden Tree") is a giant, gold-colored tree that towers above the Lands Between in Elden Ring.

## Overview

The Erdtree is the heart of the Golden Order, and a divine blessing and direct product of the Elden Ring.
The Altus Plateau was regarded as the realm of the Erdtree, and its people believed that those blessed to be born at its foot held a special significance. The blessing granted by the Erdtree and consequently the Elden Ring, is represented by a golden light in the eyes of the inhabitants of the Lands Between, known as the Grace of Gold. Upon their deaths, the remembrances of demigods and greater champions, and other such powerful beings are hewn into the Erdtree.
The entrance to the Erdtree is currently sealed by a latticework of Impenetrable Thorns in the shape of Radagon's Rune.

## Background

The Crucible of life was the primordial matter that became the Erdtree and the place where all life was once blended together. It was said to be close in nature to life itself, and was represented by gold with a reddish tint— primordial gold. Queen Marika transformed the Crucible into the Erdtree under the guidance of the Two Fingers.

### Age of the Erdtree

The birth of the Erdtree coincided with the War Against the Giants. Following the defeat of the Fire Giants and their Fell God, and the confinement of the Flame of Ruin, Queen Marika declared the beginning of a new epoch, glistening with life, known as the Age of the Erdtree.
Although the Erdtree came from a blending of all life, many cultures were deemed vulgar under Marika's regime. In the time of Lord Godfrey, everything once stood in opposition to the Erdtree. Through countless victories in war, it became the embodiment of Order, and defeated were eradicated, enslaved, or banished to the fringes.

#### Age of Plenty

When the new age began, the Erdtree flourished with abundance. It secreted primordial life energy in the form of sacred sap, which hardened into an amber that was treasured as the most precious of jewels. These blessings fell like golden tears from its boughs, pooling in flasks or large collection basins. Queen Marika herself personally bestowed them to her Elden Lord and chosen few. These Sacred Tears were worshipped and used to spread the faith in churches across all lands.
During these early days, the Erdtree radiated the gentle warmth of the sun and healed all who basked in its golden glow. Its bark at this time was brown, much like the Minor Erdtrees.
It was thought that the blessed sap of the Erdtree would drip from its boughs forever, but that era of blind belief swiftly came to an end. It was only for a fleeting moment, as is the course of all life. Over time, the Erdtree became more an object of faith.

#### Erdtree Burial

Erdtree burial was an honor awarded to heroes as a proper death meant returning to the Erdtree. The remains of such champions were deposited within the catacombs built around the Greattree roots that once connected to the Erdtree. Indeed there were once heroes abundantly blessed by the Erdtree who upon earning their honors simply died. Statues of Rosus, Usher of Death, were erected in order to guide the dead who lose their way during their return to the Erdtree roots.

### Age of the Elden Ring

On the Night of Black Knives, a stolen fragment of the Rune of Death was used to murder Godwyn the Golden. As a hero and the first demigod to die, Godwyn's body was buried deep beneath Leyndell in the Deeproot Depths overlooking the Nameless Eternal City. Although his soul has perished, his body did not, and his Erdtree burial enabled the Rune of Death to spread throughout the great tree roots, sprouting in the form of Deathroot across the Lands Between. Deathroot violated the catacombs and gave rise to Those Who Live in Death, who refused the Erdtree's call to return. At some point, either before or after the Elden Ring was shattered, the connection between the Erdtree and the great tree roots was severed.

### The Shattering

Minor Erdtrees were considered to be the offspring of the Erdtree. After the Elden Ring was shattered, golden seeds flew from the Erdtree and scattered across the Lands Between, as if life itself knew that its end had come. It used to be believed that Erdtree seeds could not exist, as the Erdtree was once perfect and eternal. Erdtree Avatars emerged following the shattering of the Elden Ring, determined to protect the withering Erdtree's offspring. Illusory trees, or phantom trees, also grew from the scattered golden seeds. Additionally, the Wrath of Gold incantation was discovered, and feared as a sign of the Erdtree's wrath.
Queen Marika was imprisoned inside the Erdtree after she shattered the Elden Ring. At some point following the shattering of the Elden Ring, the entrance to the Erdtree was blocked by impenetrable thorns, emblazoned with the seal of King Consort Radagon, which prevent access to anyone outside. The Flame of Ruin was capable of burning the Erdtree and destroying the impenetrable thorns, but doing so was long held to be the first cardinal sin.

### Game events

After the defeat of Morgott, the Omen King, The Tarnished attempted to enter the Erdtree to repair the Elden Ring, become Marika's consort, and ascend as Elden Lord. However, they were unable to enter due to the impenetrable thorns. The Tarnished then traveled to the Mountaintops of the Giants to rekindle the Flame of Ruin and burn the Erdtree, either by sacrificing Melina (as was her duty as the kindling maiden) or by using the Frenzied Flame. This journey eventually led them to Crumbling Farum Azula, where the defeat of Maliketh, the Black Blade unbound the Rune of Death, enabling them to enter the burning Erdtree.
