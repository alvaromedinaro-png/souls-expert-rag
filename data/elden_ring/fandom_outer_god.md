# Outer God
Verdigris is said to be the gift of an outer god. Possessed of an enormously hefty yet supple strength thanks to its rusted nature. - Verdigris Armor
The Outer Gods ( 外なる神々, Sotonaru Kamigami) are unseen, enigmatic entities wielding immense power and influence over the world of Elden Ring, often acting indirectly to reshape the Lands Between according to their inscrutable designs. They manifest through various natural or supernatural phenomena, often tied to one or more divine elements.

## List of Outer Gods


### Formless Mother

The Formless Mother, also known as the Mother of Truth, is an arcane outer god associated with Omen, hornsent, blood, and bloodflame. She craves wounds and is said to bestow power upon those born with accursed blood. The Blood Oath incantations channel her divine element, accessed by puncturing her omnipresent body with ritual spears. It is believed that the eternal blood of the Formless Mother will never dry or turn to rot.
Her current followers are Mohg, the Lord of Blood; his Mohgwyn Dynasty; and the bloodfiends, former hornsent.

### Outer God of Death

An outer god of Death once held influence over the Lands Between. Its powers presumably included Death Blight, Spectral Light, and the Ghostflame wielded by the ancient Deathbirds and Gravebirds. This entity may also be connected to spirit -calling hexes.
The Twinbird and White-Winged Maidens are said to be the envoys of Death. After his burial at the roots of the Erdtree, Godwyn the Golden was transformed into the Prince of Death.

### Outer God of Frenzied Flame

This theoretical outer god is representated by the maddening, yellow Flame of Frenzy; however, it is unclear whether the Frenzied Flame itself embodies this divinity or is simply a manifestation of its power. Nonetheless, its purpose is to catalyze the reunification of the One Great by destroying the Lands Between and plunging the world into chaos. It presents profound danger to all life, possessing the ability to melt away even eternal spirits. The god is associated with the Eye of Yelough and contagious yellow embers in the eyes of its followers.
The Flame of Frenzy initially entered the world through impressions left upon the grave of an ancient god by the Readerless Fingers, likely in the Finger Ruins. Later, its use was forbidden and vehemently suppressed by the Hornsent, Queen Marika, and Melina. Its blind Finger Maiden later described its envoy as a "distant light," visible only after amassing numerous Shabriri Grapes.
The Three Fingers serve as the corporeal agents of the Frenzied Flame. Its Chaos Followers — Hyetta, Midra, Nanaya, Shabriri, Vyke, and the immured Great Caravan —await the Lord of Frenzy, who will ignite the Erdtree with the yellow flame.

### Outer God of Night

An outer god of Night is thought to be the 'greater being' behind the Nightlord in Elden Ring Nightreign. Its power is the Night's Tide that threatens Limveld. This god is identified by bones with a distinct lifelessness, which manifest in areas under its influence, such as the central castle and beyond the Spirit Shelter gate. The Iron Menial received one such Bone-Like Stone from the Nightlord in a dream, granting it to the Recluse when he awoke. Though it held the power to expunge divine essence, it could also be destroyed by it. It also held the power to alter fate, affirming a connection to the primeval current and stars.
- An eye appears in skybox for each Nightlords' arena, and is formed by the clouds above the final ring on both nights in Limveld.
- The "distinct lifelessness," "dead of night," and association with bones may imply a connection to the outer god of death, who is already connected to Night. The Black Knifeprint is composed of starlight.

### Outer God of Rot

The outer god of Scarlet Rot whose essence was sealed away by a blind swordsman in the Lake of Rot. A great scorpion's tail, which was crafted into a dagger, is said to be a relic of this outer god. Miquella's Needle was crafted and inserted into Malenia, Blade of Miquella to ward off the affliction of the Scarlet Rot.
Its current followers include Romina, Saint of the Bud; Sage Gowry; and the Servants of Rot.
According to Gowry's understanding, the Laws of an Order of Rot would be those of "Decay" (腐敗の律) and "Rebirth" (輪廻の理).

## List of Unconfirmed Outer Gods

This section lists the entities with a very similar role to the outer gods, but are never textually referred to as such.

### Fell God

The nameless god of fire is the source of the Giants' Flame and Giants' Flame incantations; however, it interferes with the Lands Between more directly. It is an ancient, malformed deity that was depicted with curled horns and thick braids of bright red hair. It is also tied to smithing and verdigris.

### Blood Star

The blood star is a mysterious entity responsible for Briars of Punishment and Briars of Sin, two aberrant Thorn Sorceries. These sorceries require Faith to use, which implies the blood star is something that can be worshiped, and is therefore an entity. The Guilty could only see the blood star after they were blinded.

### Serpent-God

The ancient inhabitants of Mt. Gelmir made sacrifices in the name of a Serpent-God. It is likely connected to one of the god-like snakes, such as the Abyssal Serpent and God-Devouring Serpent.
