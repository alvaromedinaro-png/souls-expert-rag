# Beasthood
Beasthood is a stat in Bloodborne.

## Mechanics

Transformation status can be obtained by consuming a Beast Blood Pellet or using the transformed version of Beast Claw, and is further enhanced by certain sets of attire.
When Transformed, a bonus to physical attack is received, with a penalty to all defenses. The Transformation bar will fill up as attacking persists. The higher the bar, the higher the attack bonus and defense penalty.
- The amount of Insight a player has will directly decrease his/her Beasthood bar, making it smaller.
- Slower, heavier attacks fill the Transformation bar faster than a flurry of lighter attacks.
- Hitting multiple enemies will increase the bar faster.

## Levels of Beasthood

The length of the Transformation is dependent on how high the Beasthood stat is, and the Beasthood buff will cap at a value of 300. Further increases to the Beasthood bar will only aid in keeping it at that capped value until it virtually starts declining once more.
- 1 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.2/ DEF DOWN x0.8
- 25 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.3/ DEF DOWN x0.7
- 50 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.4/ DEF DOWN x0.6
- 100 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.5/ DEF DOWN x0.5
- 200 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.6/ DEF DOWN x0.4
- 300 Beasthood- PHYS ATK UP x1.7/ DEF DOWN x0.2
